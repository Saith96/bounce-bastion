Bounce Bastion Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Support

What the game stores

Bounce Bastion saves your settings, best scores, shards and Workshop progress only on your device. We do not run servers, require an account, or receive this data.

Purchases

The optional "Full Game" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether you own the unlock.

Advertising

Bounce Bastion shows ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional; interstitial ads appear only between runs and never for players who bought the Full Game.

Google may collect device and usage information, such as your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP-based coarse location, ad interactions and crash diagnostics, to serve and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS you can allow or deny tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Bounce Bastion is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
