How to play

Easy to pick up, hard to put down

  1. Slide your paddle

    Drag anywhere on the screen. Your orbs fire on their own: ricochet them off walls, blocks and your paddle.

  2. Build your run

    Level up and pick from three upgrades. Stack them into Evolutions, reroll a bad hand, and take down the boss.

  3. Come back stronger

    Every run earns shards, win or lose. Spend them in the Workshop on 12 permanent perks.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I restore my Full Game purchase?

Open Settings in the game and tap “Restore purchase”, or use the button on the unlock screen.

Does buying the Full Game remove ads?

Yes, it removes all interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available but are always optional.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, tracking permission can also be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, manage your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, you can play offline.
