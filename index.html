Bounce Bastion

A one-thumb ricochet roguelite for iPhone. Slide your paddle, bounce your orbs and hold the line through ten chapters of bosses and a new Daily Rift every day.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email saithdevelopments@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I restore my Full Game purchase? Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchase", or use the button on the unlock screen.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the game, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. Tracking permission can also be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does buying the Full Game remove ads? Yes, it removes all interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available but are always optional.
